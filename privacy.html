Privacy Policy

YesterChat

Last updated: September 1, 2025

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how YesterChat collects, uses, and protects your personal information. We are committed to transparency and giving you control over your data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create a YesterChat account, we collect:

  • Username (chosen by you)
  • Email address (for account verification and important notifications)
  • Password (encrypted and stored securely)
  • Account creation date and last login information

1.2 Message Content

YesterChat processes and stores:

  • Public Messages: Messages sent in public channels are stored on our servers and visible to other users in those channels
  • Direct Messages (DMs): Private messages between users are encrypted and stored securely
  • Message Metadata: Timestamps, sender/receiver information, and delivery status

Important Notice About Direct Messages: While we respect your privacy, direct messages (DMs) may be accessed and reviewed by our moderation team when reported by users for violations of our Terms of Service or Community Guidelines. This access is limited to investigating reported content and ensuring user safety. We do not routinely monitor private conversations.

1.3 Technical Information

We automatically collect certain technical information:

  • IP address and general location information
  • Device type, browser information, and operating system
  • Connection timestamps and usage patterns
  • Error logs and performance data

1.4 Optional Information

You may choose to provide additional information such as:

  • Profile picture or avatar
  • Status messages or bio
  • Preferences and settings

2. How We Use Your Information

Purpose Information Used Legal Basis
Provide messaging service Account info, messages, technical data Contract performance
Account security and verification Email, password, IP address Legitimate interest
Content moderation and safety Reported messages, user behavior Legitimate interest
Service improvement Usage patterns, error logs Legitimate interest
Legal compliance Any information as required Legal obligation

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

3.1 General Policy

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared in the limited circumstances described below.

3.2 When We May Share Information

  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or regulatory authority
  • Safety and Security: To protect users from harm, prevent illegal activity, or enforce our terms
  • Business Transfers: If YesterChat is acquired or merged, user data may be transferred as part of the transaction
  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party services that help us operate (hosting, email delivery, analytics) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • User Reports: Reported content may be shared with our moderation team for review

3.3 Public Information

The following information is visible to other YesterChat users:

  • Username and profile picture
  • Messages posted in public channels
  • Online/offline status (if not disabled)
  • Any information you choose to share publicly

4. Data Security and Protection

4.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • End-to-end encryption for direct messages
  • Secure password hashing using bcrypt
  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and staff training on data protection

4.2 Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you within 72 hours and provide details about what happened and what we're doing to address it.

5. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

5.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Objection: Object to certain types of data processing

5.2 Account Settings

You can control various privacy settings through your account:

  • Online status visibility
  • Who can send you direct messages
  • Email notification preferences
  • Data sharing preferences

5.3 Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile and personal information are permanently removed
  • Your messages in public channels may remain but will be anonymized
  • Direct messages are deleted from our servers
  • Some information may be retained for legal or security purposes as required

6. Data Retention

6.1 Retention Periods

We retain your information for different periods depending on the type:

  • Account Information: Until you delete your account
  • Messages: Until you delete them or your account (except as noted below)
  • Moderation Records: Up to 2 years for safety and compliance
  • Technical Logs: Up to 90 days for troubleshooting and security
  • Legal Holds: As required by law or pending legal proceedings

7. Children's Privacy

YesterChat is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that we have collected such information, we will delete it immediately. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

YesterChat may store and process your data in countries other than your own. We ensure that any international transfers comply with applicable privacy laws and that your data receives adequate protection through appropriate safeguards.

9. Cookies and Tracking

9.1 Cookies We Use

YesterChat uses cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic site functionality and security
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our service

9.2 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling essential cookies may affect your ability to use YesterChat.

10. Third-Party Services

YesterChat may integrate with third-party services for functionality like:

  • Email delivery services
  • Analytics and performance monitoring
  • Content delivery networks

These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will:

  • Post the updated policy on this page
  • Update the "Last updated" date
  • Notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification
  • Give you the opportunity to review and accept major changes

12. Contact Us

Questions about your privacy or this policy?

We're here to help. Contact us through:

  • Email: yestermesterbusiness@gmail.com

We will respond to your privacy inquiries within 30 days.

Your trust is important to us. We're committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices. If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out.
